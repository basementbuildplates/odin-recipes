Chicken Chili

Description

This chicken chili is amaizing. I made it for a chili cook off. It took 1st place! It smells so good while it's cooking. This is the only chicken chili recipe you will ever need.

Ingredients

  • 1lb chicken breast chopped
  • 2tbsp dried minced onion
  • 1tbsp olive oil
  • 1tsp dried minced garlic
  • 2 14 oz. cans chicken broth
  • 1 4oz. can chopped green chilis
  • 2 tsp ground cumin - or more if you like a little more flavor
  • 2 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 1/2 tsp cayenne pepper
  • 3 15 oz. cans great northern beans

Instructions

  1. Cook chicken and onion in oil until lightly browned
  2. Add garlic and cook for 1 minute
  3. Stir in broth, chilis,cumin,oregano,and cayenne
  4. Reduce heat to low
  5. Mash 1 can of beans until smooth
  6. Add to chili
  7. Add the rest of the beans to the chili
  8. Simmer until thickened - The longer it cooks the more flavorful it becomes
  9. Serve with monterey jack cheese
  10. Enjoy!
