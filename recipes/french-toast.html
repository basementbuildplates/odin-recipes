Baked French Toast Casserole

Description

This is one of my favorite breakfast or brunch dishes. It is packed full of flavor. This is a dish that gets requested often by my family for Christmas and Easter brunch.

Ingredients

  • 1 loaf french bread (13 to 16 ounces)
  • 8 large eggs
  • 2 cups half-and-half
  • 1 cup milk
  • 2 tbsp granulated sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/4 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1/4 tsp ground nutmeg
  • dash of salt
  • mapple syrup

Praline Topping

  • 1/2 pound butter
  • 1 cup packed light brown sugar
  • 1 cup chopped pecans
  • 2 tbsp light corn syrup
  • 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1/2 tsp ground nutmeg

Instructions

Casserole Prep

  1. Slice french bread into 20 1 inch slices
  2. Generously butter a 9 by 13 baking dish
  3. Arrange the slices in 2 rows, overlaping slices/li>
  4. In a large bowl, combine eggs, half-and-half, milk, sugar, vanilla, cinnamon, nutmeg, and salt
  5. Whisk until blended but not too bubbly
  6. Pour mixture over bread slices
  7. Cover slices evenly
  8. spoon mixture between slices
  9. Cover with foil and refrigerate overnight

Praline Topping

  1. Combine Praline Topping ingredients into a medium bowl and mix well
  2. Spread evenly over the top of the bread slices

Bake

  1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees F
  2. Bake for 40 minutes, until puffed and lightly golden

ENJOY!

Serve with mapple syrup
