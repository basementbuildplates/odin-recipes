Mexican Rice

Description

This is an easy mexican rice recipe. It tastes great on it own or topped with cheese and cilantro. Pairs well with many other dishes.

Ingredients

  • 3 tbsp vegitable oil
  • 1/4 cup dried minced onion
  • 1 1/2 cup uncooked rice
  • 1tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp chili powder
  • 1 8oz. can tomato sauce
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 1 1/2 tsp dried minced garlic
  • 1 cup water

Instructions

  1. Heat oil over medium-low heat
  2. Add rice and stir to coat with oil
  3. Stir in tomato sauce, cumin, chili powder, onion, garlic, and salt
  4. Add water
  5. Bring to boil
  6. Reduce heat
  7. Simmer for 40 minutes
  8. Enjoy!
